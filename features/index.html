Everything Sāmwaad does

Four core pieces work together: live captioning, group sessions, scheduled check-ins, and a searchable history. Each one below includes what it does, why it matters, and how it's built.

Quick Captioning

One tap turns spoken conversation into live, readable captions — color-coded by speaker, updating in real time as people talk.

  • Real-time speech-to-text via Apple's on-device Speech framework
  • Speaker-aware, color-coded caption bubbles
  • Wraps up into an AI-generated summary automatically
Under the hood:
  • Captioning runs on Apple's on-device SFSpeechRecognizer.
  • Speaker identification uses a custom CoreML model that extracts a voice embedding and matches it against stored profiles via cosine similarity.
  • Profiles adapt over time through rolling-average updates.
  • A retroactive "Time Machine" correction engine can re-classify earlier segments if a speaker is renamed.

Group Conversations

Bring more than one person into a session. Create a room and share a short code, or join one that's already running — captions sync live for everyone.

  • Instant room codes — no accounts required to join
  • Captions and summaries sync across every participant
  • Built for classrooms, meetings, and family conversations
Under the hood:
  • Sessions sync through Firebase Realtime Database.
  • Each room is identified by a short, auto-generated code.
  • Messages and presence update live for every participant connected to that room.

Quick Actions

Recurring conversations don't need setting up from scratch every time. Choose participants, a start time, the days it repeats, and a category once — Sāmwaad handles the reminders.

  • Repeats on custom days — e.g. Mon / Wed / Fri
  • Organized by category — Office, Family, Friends
  • Local notifications ahead of each scheduled session
Under the hood:
  • Quick actions are stored locally and scheduled with iOS local notifications ahead of each session.
  • Cross-device presence is synced through Firebase so status stays current wherever you check it.

View Conversations

Every session is saved automatically. Browse your history at a glance, then reopen any conversation to replay the transcript or revisit its AI summary.

  • Full transcript and AI summary saved per session
  • History organized by date
  • Export any summary as a PDF to share
Under the hood:
  • Conversations, messages, and voice profiles persist locally via SwiftData.
  • Post-session summaries use on-device Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models) where supported.
  • Any summary can be exported as a PDF and shared through the iOS share sheet.

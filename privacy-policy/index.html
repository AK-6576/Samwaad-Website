Summary

Sāmwaad is built so that your voice stays on your device wherever possible.

  • On-device audio processing. Voice audio captured for live captioning and speaker calibration is processed locally, in memory. We never stream, record, or upload raw audio to a server.
  • On-device speaker recognition. A local model (VL1004) computes anonymous voice embeddings directly on your device's hardware.
  • Encrypted sync. Group session transcripts synced to our cloud database are encrypted on your device (AES-256) before they're sent.
  • No ad tracking. We don't sell your data, show third-party ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

What we collect and how it's used

Microphone & spoken audio

Used to power live captioning and on-device speaker identification. Audio is buffered briefly in memory to run Apple's speech recognizer and our local model, then discarded — it's never saved to disk or uploaded.

Voice calibration profile

Calibration extracts a numerical voice embedding representing your vocal characteristics, stored locally in your device's private storage. You can delete or redo this at any time in Settings → Vocal Profile.

Account information

If you create an account, we collect your email and authentication credentials via Firebase Authentication or Google Sign-In, used to manage your identity and sync sessions across devices.

Group sessions & transcripts

Text transcripts, participant names, action items, and session summaries. Synced data is protected with AES-256 client-side encryption — the keys live in your device's Keychain and are never accessible to us in plain text. A local copy is also kept on-device for offline review.

Location (optional)

If you enable it, Sāmwaad uses reverse geocoding to add a city or venue name to auto-generated summaries. This only runs during an active session, and only with "While Using the App" permission.

On-device & Apple Intelligence

On supported hardware, Sāmwaad uses on-device Apple Intelligence (Foundation Models) to generate post-session summaries, action items, and notes. All of this runs on your device — conversation transcripts are never sent to an external AI service to produce these summaries.

Third-party services

Sāmwaad relies on a small set of trusted frameworks:

  • Apple Speech framework & CoreML — on-device speech recognition and voice processing, governed by Apple.
  • Firebase Authentication & Realtime Database (Google) — account login and encrypted group-session sync, governed by Google's Cloud Privacy Notice.

We don't use third-party advertising SDKs or trackers.

Retention & your controls

You're in control of your data at all times:

  • Delete your voice profile under Profile → Vocal Profile → Delete Profile.
  • Clear conversation history from the View Conversations screen at any time.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Delete Account, or by contacting support. This immediately and permanently removes your authentication record, cloud data, and local records on your device.

Children's privacy

Sāmwaad does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
